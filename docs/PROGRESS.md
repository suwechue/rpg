## How to check

- Tests: `& $godot --headless --path . -s res://tests/run_tests.gd` (exit 0 = pass). Suites live in `tests/suites/*.gd` and extend `tests/suite.gd`; `run_tests.gd` runs its own `test_` methods and then every suite.
- Load check: `& $godot --headless --path . --quit`.
- Screenshots (needs a window, not headless): `& $godot --path . -- --scene=res://... --debug --shot=C:/Users/itzju/Documents/project-vector/tools/screenshots/name.png --frames=30` (handled in `DebugMenu._dev_args`). Save screenshots in `tools/screenshots/` (gitignored).
- Scene smoke tests (autoloads + real scenes): `& $godot --headless --path . -s res://tests/smoke_scenes.gd` (exit 0 = pass). Add a `smoke_*` method per scene.
- Dev flags after `--`: `--seed=N --class=mage` (fresh run), `--jump=week,day_index,part_index`, `--town=zone:x,y`, `--scene=res://...`, `--autoplay` (AI plays the party), `--debug`, `--shot=PATH --frames=N`.
- F1 opens the debug menu in any scene.

## Decisions made during development

- **PATCH_1 conflicts (Oct 28):** 2 Actions + 1 Quick replaces AR's move-or-act and CU's Move + 1 Action; hand of 3 refreshed each turn with Hold replaces CT Part 4's cycling; the double-attack chance replaces CT's move-and-act chance; no deck maximum replaces deckMaxBase 20; Strike and Guard are no longer deck cards. PATCH_1 wins each time.
- **Git:** `origin` is https://github.com/suwechue/rpg.git; every milestone commit is pushed. Art and audio under `assets/` are tracked with Git LFS.
- **Assets imported** with `godot --headless --import`; the `.import` files are committed. The pack font imports with antialiasing, hinting and subpixel positioning off.
- **UI font:** NormalFont.ttf has a 227.5-unit pixel grid (2048 per em), so its native size is 9px (18 for titles). Its space is 1px, so the theme uses a FontVariation with +2 space width (`assets/ui/theme.tres`, set as the project theme because theme inheritance stops at Node2D parents).
- **Starter decks are 10 cards** for every class (the Mage's "8-card deck" plus Strike and 2 Guard) so they meet the 10-card deck minimum.
- **Known Vectors ignore the Knowledge Tier.** The tier caps Magnitude and which new Vectors and Forms level-up rolls can teach. Class kits that start with a Tier 2 Vector (Healer: Light) can use it from day one.
- **Form tiers:** Tier 1: Bolt, Burst, Shield, Weapon Infusion, Touch, Projectile, Line, Aura (every class kit uses only these). Tier 2: Sphere, Construct, Glyph.
- **Knowledge Tier rises** at level 5 with INT 8 (Tier 2) and level 10 with INT 12 (Tier 3) (`data/leveling.json`). Boss rewards can also raise it (M8).
- **Leveling details** (ported from Nexus): XP to next level = 20 x level; a Vector learning roll every level and a Form roll on even levels; a known axis rolled again gains +2 affinity; pity after 3 dry rolls per table.
- **HP growth:** derived formula plus a per-class `hpPerLevel` (Mage 1, Fighter 3, others 2). MP grows only through stats.
- **Hand limit:** draws stop at 7 cards; the extra cards stay in the draw pile.
- **Combat rules picked where the design was open:** range is Manhattan distance; damaging AoEs only hit the caster's opponents (and props), healing only hits allies (no friendly fire). Line and Projectile aim in the 4 grid directions. Units block movement; downed party members stay on the grid as bodies and can be revived by any heal.
- **Turn order:** each turn draws 2 cards (including the first, so a full opening turn has 7). Statuses (slow, root, blind, haste, rally) last through the unit's next turn. Tile effects and constructs count down per round.
- **Forms:** Shield absorbs Magnitude for 2 of the target's turns; a Light or Vitality Shield also heals 80% of Magnitude. Aura follows the caster and ticks at the start of each of their turns for 3 turns (cast turn included). Construct is a sight-blocking barrier with HP = Magnitude for 3 rounds. Glyph is a trap for 6 rounds that fires the spell on an opponent who steps on it. Weapon Infusion adds half the Magnitude to the next 2 Strikes plus the Vector's Contest and effect.
- **Vector effects:** Heat burns tiles 2 rounds (3 damage on entry or turn start) and makes water into steam; Cold slows 1 turn and freezes water to ice; Force pushes 1 tile per 10 Magnitude (min 1), 4 collision damage to both; Motion pulls enemies 1 tile and gives allies +2 Movement; Binding roots; Light blinds (35% miss) and heals with Shield/Aura; Transmutation clears hazards and water; Vitality drains 50%; Corrosion shreds 2 Defense and 4 Magic Resistance.
- **Instability failures** are a weighted Sample: Fizzle 4, Backlash 3 (half damage to the caster), Misfire 2 (random valid target), Wild Surge 1 (random Vector from all 9, same target).
- **Perks in combat:** Overchannel (first improvise at normal cost), Second Wind (auto at <30% HP), Backstab (+50% from behind or a different row within 1 column), Triage (-1 MP on heals for allies under half HP), Hype (+4 initiative and +1 draw on turn 1 for the whole party).
- **Guests call for help** (Kelly, M6): when hurt below 60% or next to an enemy at the start of their turn. The call is answered if a player-controlled unit heals or shields them, or hits an enemy next to them, before their next turn.
- **Units are drawn at 2x** (integer scale) in combat; bosses at 1x since their sheets are already 50 to 60 px.
- **Stat training bars:** a stat point costs 3 bars below 7, 4 from 7, 5 from 13 (`stats.json` "training"). A class gives 1 bar to each of its stats; repeating a class the same day gives half. Pop quiz success repeats the class gains. Class checks are d6 + stat against a DC.
- **Grade** is per professor (Ms. G and Mr. Mustard each teach two classes): +3 per attendance, -5 per skip, 0 to 100. Spotlight events need Grade 25 to 40.
- **Sleep restores HP and MP fully** at the end of each day. HP lost to class accidents carries into that evening's dungeon. MP only refills by sleeping, rest rooms, food and per-turn regen.
- **Injuries:** a member downed at a fight's end is injured for 2 day-ends, so they miss the next evening's dungeon.
- **Detention** (3 skips in a week) takes the next After School: a Mr. B scene, then AP 0.
- **Crafting:** input vector = design direction x Magnitude power. Performance below 0.35 rolls no Properties, below 0.8 one, else two; 0.95+ adds a Magnitude step (capped by Tier). Designs without Properties live in the collection by spell id; designs with Properties are stored as `craft_N`. Property effects: power, powerPct, costDelta, stability, critPct.
- **Card collection** is shared; each deck may use up to the owned count (max 3 per design). Party members can also use their own starter cards. Spell cards need the deck owner to know the card's Vectors and Form.
- **The worked-example test** for properties now filters to the doc's three sample properties, since the Property Bank grew.
- **Town layout:** Residential is one zone with Hillside at the north end, Elm Street in the middle and Maple Street in the south; it joins downtown on its east edge at Elm Street (TOWN.md's diagram can't hold both "Hillside north of downtown" and "one zone"). The abandoned mansion sits in the outskirts zone as the table says.
- **Interiors** are small rooms with Ninja Adventure floor/brick tiles and drawn placeholder furniture. School classrooms are rooms off one hallway interior. The corner store stays EXTERIOR but sells through a walk-up window, so it can be one of the four shops.
- **Shops** cost 1 AP per visit. Price = base x (1 + keeper Greed / 200) x (1 - (CHA - 5) x 0.02). New gear auto-equips into an empty slot; combat item cards go straight into the deck if there's room. Weapons add Strike power (`strikePower` mod), armor adds Defense.
- **Talking** across a counter or desk works (shopkeepers stand behind them).
- **Reception:** score = Match(current disposition, the line's tone) + mood (good +0.15, bad -0.15) + Bond/400, clamped to -1..1. Good at 0.2+, bad at -0.05 or less; Bond change = score x 10 (x up to 1.45 for risky lines when it lands), neutral +1. A misheard line uses its alternate's tone (or the flipped tone) and can never land as good.
- **Topics** cost 1 AP and can each be used once per week (some only once ever). Moods are seeded per NPC per day. Gifts: +5 per liked tag +5 (max 15), disliked -5, otherwise +2. Hanging out (2 AP, Acquaintance+) gives +6..+12.
- **Recruitment pacing:** Core Four from week 1; Sam, Baxter and Jon from week 2; Kris, Diane, Penny from week 3 (Jake also needs his forge questline, Phase 3 deli shifts); Mel from week 4. Kelly needs Bond 60 and a kind first night (Risk 0 or less). Recruits join near your level minus one. The roster holds anyone; 3 come along each night.
- **Kelly's first night:** the evening is a scripted tutorial floor (story scene) rather than a generated one. Risk sources match DESIGN.md exactly; the panic choice reassurances need CHA 5 or having defended her earlier. The tutorial fight is two Detention Slips so it stays short. Surviving sets Kelly's Bond by (3 - Risk) x 4 and, at Risk 0 or less, her Loyalty to 60 and her recruitment flag.
- **Narrator tone:** Rival or worse = sarcastic, Stranger = dry, Acquaintance and up = warm. The narrator is an NPC at the park and woods edge with their own topics (two hint at identities without confirming).
- **Floors:** the DESIGN.md room counts (6/7/8/9) include the start and exit rooms. Doors connect every pair of adjacent rooms. The exit is the room farthest from the start; you can leave as soon as you reach it. Friday is a line: start, 3 returning fights, rest, boss, exit.
- **Returning encounters:** fled-from fights first (x1.25 x1.15 power), then the most recent wins (x1.25), then fresh picks.
- **Party scaling:** smaller parties face fewer, weaker enemies: solo half the enemies at 75% power, two at 75% count and 90%, three normal, four 110%. Week 1 fights have at most 2 enemies before scaling. Bosses always appear.
- **Entering:** standing within 6 tiles of the woods edge at dusk lets you pick the party; otherwise you're pulled in with your current party. Everyone starts in the start room (the "random room" part of the design was simplified).
- **Shards:** 3 shards of an axis teach that Vector if the Knowledge Tier allows. Treasure: money x week, maybe an item, a combat card or a shard of the week's signature.
- **Boss rewards:** week 1 teaches a random new Form (Burst, Projectile, Line or Touch); weeks 2 and 4 raise the Knowledge Tier to 2 and 3.
- **The Healer starts with a Light Bolt** (3 copies instead of 2 Light Jabs and 1 Aura) so a solo Healer can finish fights.
- **Journal unlocks** are sidegrade starting traits (Night Owl, Clear Speaker, Early Bird, Warm Hands, Cold Feet), never raw power.
- **Balance snapshot (AI plays, 20 seeds, week 1 nights, Tue/Wed/Thu/Fri survival):** solo Fighter 16/17/17/15, solo Mage 9/13/10/3, with one ally most classes 12-20 on weekdays and 5-15 on Friday. The AI never flees or leaves early; a player can.
- **XP curve:** 45 x level XP per level (was 20 in Nexus) so a run ends around level 12 to 14, as DESIGN.md targets.
- **After a won fight** everyone standing recovers 20% of max HP (MP still doesn't refill, per Section 18). Without it the AI party died on about 10% of nights.
- **Encounter scaling** is gentler by week (x1, x1, x1.15, x1.35, x1.55, x1.75 for weeks 0-5) and by party size (solo 75% power, two 82%, three 90%, four 100%).
- **Advanced classes:** Kinetic Logician also accepts Fighters (Kris's dossier), Idol's "befriended the cheer squad" is Bond 20 with Kara and Kayla, Deli Knight's "Gary bond tier 3" is Friend (41+), Smith-Mage's apprentice flag comes from offering to help the blacksmith. At level 12 you can take Upside-Touched or any Tier 2 class you skipped. Perk levels 6 and 9 keep offering your starting class's perk list.
- **Week 2 story beat** brings the Core Four to your lunch table (+10 Bond each if you ask them to stick together), which keeps recruitment reachable in week 2 as Section 18 asks.
- **The finale** goes straight from week 5's boss floor to NARRATOR_REVEAL_TBD and the ending; the run is over and the save is cleared.
- **Magic Resistance as a percent:** each point of Magic Resistance is 2% spell damage reduction, capped at 60% (`stats.json` combat block).
