# Act 2 (design stub)

Act 1 ends after week 5: the fountain explodes during the dance, the party fights the Mirror Town in the square, and the aftermath weekend closes with "End of Act 1". The save is kept so Act 2 can continue it.

## What changes

- **Daylight fights.** The Upside-Down leaks into the real town. Leak points (started in the Act 1 aftermath, `data/town/changes.json` kind "leak") become a regular part of the day: they open and close by week, and sealing them matters to the town.
- **A new town state.** The fountain is a glowing crater, there's a curfew, the buses stop at six, and some shops close. Mr. B is either gone (exposed) or hiding in plain sight (covered up), depending on Act 1.
- **New locations open:** the train station (lit for the first time in thirty years), the abandoned mansion (a window that opens and closes), the old factory (Kenney Tiny Factory art is reserved for it), and the lake and boathouse.
