**Changes from the last pass, per your latest notes:**
- The four core "one per class" recruitable students (Theo, Mara, Dex, Priya) are now genuinely **new** characters, not reused MSS personalities — Theo, Dex, and Priya are freshly written below (Mara already was new). This avoids overlap between them and the MSS cast.
- All the MSS kids (Sam, Baxter, Jon, Kris, Diane, Penny, Jake, Phase, Mel) are now framed as a separate pool of **new recruitable side characters** — fully playable, each with a distinct combat hook, but explicitly a tier below the four "class rep" students rather than competing with them for the same narrative slots.
- Teachers still overlap with MSS as before — no change there.
- Sam's hidden unmasked class is renamed to **Complete Axiomatic Mage**, and per your instruction I'm leaving its actual design (Vectors, stats, traits, full description) blank/TBD rather than inventing details — just the name, the concept, and the discovery conditions are locked in for now.

## <Kelly>  (Fixed protagonist-adjacent character)  [FIXED — not reused from MSS]

**Summary:** The popular, sharp-tongued cheerleader who gets pulled into the Upside-Down with the player on night one — the inciting incident of the whole game.
**Appearance:** Sleek high ponytail, letterman-style cheer jacket over the uniform, sprite silhouette instantly readable in a crowd scene; portrait has a default smirk.
**Personality:** Confident to the point of cruelty on the surface, but the Loyalty spike once earned is real and permanent — she doesn't do anything halfway, including caring about someone.
**Voice:** Clipped, sarcastic, talks over people. Sample lines: "Wow, you're STILL here." / "...okay, actually, thank you. Don't make it weird."

**Stats:** STR 6, DEX 9, CON 7, INT 8, PER 10, WIL 9, CHA 12
**Combat class:** Bard → Idol
**Vectors:** Light, Force
**Disposition (resting):** Courage 20, Greed 10, Curiosity 15, Loyalty 60 (once earned, 5 before), Kindness −30, Pride 70
**Fixed traits:** "Panics under real pressure" (if treated badly on night one, she causes a party-wipe risk), "Reads a room instantly" (+2 PER in social scenes), "All or nothing" (Bond moves in bigger increments than usual, both directions)
**Starting Bond with player:** −10

**Where to find them:**
| Day part | Location |
| --- | --- |
| Before School | Front steps, cheer squad cluster |
| Period 1-3 | PE / Drama (rotates) |
| Lunch | Cafeteria, center table |
| After School | Field (cheer practice) |
| Evening | The Upside-Down (story-locked, week 1) |
| Weekend | Town square, arcade |

**Teaches / mentors:** None directly, but gatekeeps the cheer squad's social approval
**Recruitable:** Yes — high Bond only (41+), after the player has stabilized her panic on night one without retaliating

**Dialogue topics (3-5):**
- "Compliment her cheer routine". Tone: Kindness/Pride. CHA needed: none. Good reaction: genuine (rare) warmth. Misheard as: she assumes you're mocking her.
- "Ask about the Upside-Down". Tone: Curiosity/Courage. CHA needed: 6. Good reaction: she admits she's scared, fast, then denies saying it.
- "Joke about the cheer squad". Tone: Curiosity. CHA needed: none. Good reaction (high Curiosity): laughs despite herself.
- "Offer to walk her home". Tone: Kindness. CHA needed: 8. Good reaction: flustered, deflects hard.

**Likes / dislikes (gifts):** Likes: anything with her squad's colors, actually good coffee. Dislikes: anything that reads as pity.
**Story hooks:** Week 1 inciting incident. Recruitable mid-late game. Unbreakable Bond arc: she confronts why her whole identity is built on being liked.
**Upside-Down version:** The Hollow Mascot (week 1 boss) is implied to be built from the town's collective anxiety around her and the cheer squad's social dominance.
