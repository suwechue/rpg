# Changelog

The version shows on the title screen (data/demo.json "version").

## 0.2.0 (Act 1 complete)

- **Act 1 to the end:** the school year (class cutscenes, events, the calendar, clubs, exams and the report card), the living town (generated townsfolk, party banter, a town that remembers), the phone, 38 friendship scenes, the mystery board, unlock waves, part-time jobs, the Upside-Down as a mirror town, and the finale: the fountain, the Mirror Town in the square, the aftermath weekend and End of Act 1.
- **Modes:** Permadeath, Narrator's Mercy and Story; the difficulty ramps in from week 1 and arena wave 1; flanking and isolation.
- **The arena:** bosses every 5 waves with gimmicks, the merchant, relics, potions and the backpack, deck-synergy cards, a loop after wave 50.
- **Maps:** the town is built from editor scenes with markers and blocks (docs/MAP_GUIDE.md); eight house styles, decorations, day-part light.
- **Options:** volumes, text speed, screen shake, window size and fullscreen, key rebinding; achievements in the Narrator's Journal.
- **Tools:** F8 saves a feedback zip (note, screenshot, logs, save); error logs are kept in user://logs.

## 0.1.0 (the demo)

- The first week and the Hollow Mascot, the Endless Arena, Vector combos and the Compendium, your spark, the visual card builder, cutscenes, the intro and tutorial day, the thanks screen and survey.

## Before 0.1.0

- The alpha (M0 to M8) and Phase 2 groundwork (M9 to M11, the combat update, the arena, combat tuning and two playtest patches). See docs/PROGRESS.md.
