# Changelog

The version shows on the title screen (data/demo.json "version").

## 0.3.0 (Town life, gear and a balance pass)

- **Skipping class means something:** the town is awake during school hours, with people to meet, places open, favors, and a risk of getting caught. Your parents notice.
- **Town happenings:** 12 optional events at set places and times, many during class, each the only source of an exclusive card. The morning paper and the **What can I do?** panel (G) show what's on. Five new townsfolk and a bigger crowd.
- **Gear:** armor and weapon types, an Equipment screen, ore, and a blacksmith who forges, upgrades, infuses and repairs. Gear icons. Weapons boost cards that share their tag.
- **Cards:** 23 new special cards (discard pile tricks, cards that scale, exclusives). Story mode now offers new cards: a pick after each night, better after bosses, a rare one for mystery deductions, and a weekly shelf at the library.
- **Combat:** party combos at Close Bond (Together), enemies that start hidden, turret and decoy Constructs, damage previews on every attack card.
- **Class periods are real scenes:** walk the hall, then sit in the room with your classmates and the teacher at the board.
- **Balance:** Upside-Down nights have their own numbers per difficulty. No single enemy hit can take a whole health bar. Normal gets a Last Stand; on Story the fight goes on while anyone stands. In the arena: some healing between waves and a gentler first boss, so more runs reach the campfire, whose blacksmith is now open.
- **Techniques:** all 24 work now. The Alchemy line is real: brew spells into potions at night, which also opens the Alchemist class. Constructs can pulse, and a Living Foundry turret fights for you.
- **Fixes:** the name field on the new run screen works, and clicking a class no longer starts the run. Clear data on the title screen. Long text wraps instead of running off screen. Party members no longer stack on you when you enter a zone. Townsfolk walk at a calmer pace, and characters draw in the right order.

## 0.2.0 (Act 1 complete)

- **Act 1 to the end:** the school year (class cutscenes, events, the calendar, clubs, exams and the report card), the living town (generated townsfolk, party banter, a town that remembers), the phone, 38 friendship scenes, the mystery board, unlock waves, part-time jobs, the Upside-Down as a mirror town, and the finale: the fountain, the Mirror Town in the square, the aftermath weekend and End of Act 1.
- **Modes:** Permadeath, Narrator's Mercy and Story; the difficulty ramps in from week 1 and arena wave 1; flanking and isolation.
- **The arena:** bosses every 5 waves with gimmicks, the merchant, relics, potions and the backpack, deck-synergy cards, a loop after wave 50.
- **Maps:** the town is built from editor scenes with markers and blocks (docs/MAP_GUIDE.md); eight house styles, decorations, day-part light.
- **Options:** volumes, text speed, screen shake, window size and fullscreen, key rebinding; achievements in the Narrator's Journal.
- **Tools:** F8 saves a feedback zip (note, screenshot, logs, save); error logs are kept in user://logs.
